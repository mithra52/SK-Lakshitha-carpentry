WHO WE ARE

SK Lakshitha Carpentry

SK Lakshitha Carpentry has been providing trusted carpentry and interior services for more than 30 years. Our business is owned and managed by a dedicated self-worker who personally supervises every project from start to finish.

Before starting any work, we visit the customer's location, understand their requirements, take accurate measurements, prepare quotations, and suggest the best solutions based on their needs and budget.

Along with our trained workers, we complete every project with quality workmanship, honest pricing, premium materials and on-time delivery.
