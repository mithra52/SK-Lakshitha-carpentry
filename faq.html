Frequently Asked Questions

We have answered the questions that our customers ask most often.

What services do you provide?

We provide carpentry work, PVC door installation, wooden work, mosquito net work, false ceiling, welding work, polishing and alteration services.

Do you provide a free site visit?

Yes. We visit your location, understand your requirements, take measurements and provide the best solution for your project.

How can I get a quotation?

After checking the work details and materials required, we provide a clear and transparent quotation.

Do you work for homes and commercial spaces?

Yes. We provide carpentry services for houses, apartments, offices, shops and other commercial buildings.

How many years of experience do you have?

We have more than 30+ years of experience providing quality carpentry services.

How long will the work take to complete?

The completion time depends on the project size and requirements. We always focus on completing the work on time.

Which areas do you provide service?

We provide services in Chennai and nearby areas. Contact us to confirm availability.

How can I contact you?

You can contact us through Call or WhatsApp for enquiries, site visits and quotations.
