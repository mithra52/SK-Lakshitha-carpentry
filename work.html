sheryas santhosh iyer

Shreyas Santhosh Iyer is an Indian international cricketer and top-order batter who has represented India since 2017 across formats. Known for his stylish stroke play, he scored a century and fifty on his Test debut in 2021. A 2023 World Cup finalist, 2025 Champions Trophy winner, and Asia Cup 2023 champion, Iyer has captained Delhi Capitals, Kolkata Knight Riders, and Punjab Kings in the IPL, leading KKR to the 2024 title and PBKS to the 2025 final.
